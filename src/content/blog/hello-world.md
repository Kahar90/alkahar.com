This is the first post on my new minimal blog.

I built this site with a simple philosophy: **technology should get out of the way**. No heavy frameworks, no complex state management, no tracking scripts. Just plain HTML, clean typography, and words.

## Why minimal?

In my work as a Solutions Architect, I see teams constantly over-engineer solutions. We add layers of abstraction until the original problem is buried under infrastructure. This blog is my reminder that the best solutions are often the simplest ones.

- Cloud architecture and solution design
- Software engineering lessons
- Thoughts on technology and its impact
- Personal projects and experiments
